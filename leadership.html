Strategic Clarity

The 5W Framework

5 Questions

Core Premise: Ambiguity is the enemy of execution and spiritual vitality. The 5W framework (Who, What, Where, When, Why) aligns divine identity with operational cadence.

1. WHO: Identity under God & the core team entrusted to you.
2. WHAT: The clear, unambiguous mandate/deliverable.
3. WHERE: The mission field, cultural context, and relational ecosystem.
4. WHEN: Discerning Kairos (divine timing) vs. Chronos (clock time).
5. WHY: The transcendent purpose that withstands crisis and sacrifice.
Cross-links: Big.Life Action Plans • Fully Alive "Why"

Andy Stanley • Polarity Management

My Tensions (Andy Stanley)

Polarity Navigation

Core Premise: Immature leaders try to "solve" problems that are actually persistent tensions to be navigated (Andy Stanley / Polarity Management principle).

• Grace vs. Truth: High standards with unconditional love.
• Rest (Sabbath) vs. Bold Labor: Trusting sovereignty while working diligently.
• Freedom vs. Accountability: Autonomous micro-groups with doctrinal fidelity.
• Present Contentment vs. Holy Ambition: Grateful now, pressing forward.
Cross-links: We Are Church (Freedom vs Order) • Law of Navigation

Movement Engine

Big.Life (DMM Strategy)

3/3rds Process

Core Premise: Big.Life is a global Disciple Making Movement (DMM) catalyst. Shifts leaders from knowledge-accumulation to fast, radical obedience and rapid peer-to-peer multiplication.

The 3/3rds Discovery Group Architecture:
1. Look Back (1/3): Care, worship, accountability to last week's "I will", sharing stories.
2. Look Up (1/3): Scripture Discovery (What does it say? What does it teach about God & man?).
3. Look Ahead (1/3): Action "I will" statement, practicing sharing, interceding for lost friends.
Cross-links: We Are Church Micro Gatherings • 5W (Who & Where)
DMM questions & 3/3rds template
Francis Chan

We Are Church (Francis Chan)

Decentralized House Church

Core Premise: Strip away consumerist church mechanics (buildings, paid superstar pastors, expensive productions) and return to the New Testament model: small, intense, intimate fellowships where every member exercises spiritual gifts.

• Zero Overhead: 100% of resources go toward the poor and global missions.
• Unpaid Plural Elder Oversight: Shepherds hold day jobs like Paul the tentmaker.
• No Audiences, Only Ministers: Gathering in living rooms (15-25 people max).
Why It Completes the Leadership Stack:

Maxwell's 21 Laws give leadership mechanics, 5W provides strategic clarity, Fully Alive & Tensions guard the leader's internal battery—and We Are Church provides the authentic soil where disciples actually love one another sacrificially.

Cross-links: Multiply (Discipleship) • Big.Life (Movement)
